# Receiver VR

A standalone VR build of **Receiver**, Wolfire Games' 7-day FPS experiment in realistic gun handling. Armed with a handgun and a cassette player, you explore a procedurally generated complex of turrets and shock drones while uncovering the secrets of the Mindkill.

## What you get
- Detailed simulations of a Colt 1911 pistol, S&W Model 10 "Victory" revolver, and Glock 17 with full-auto mod
- Taser robots and machine-gun turrets with physically-based damage models
- Ballistic modeling of ricochets and bullet drop
- 11 audio tapes to prepare you to defend yourself against the inevitable Mindkill event
- Become literate in how guns actually operate

## Requirements
- SteamVR
- Motion controllers

> This is a **self-contained** VR build - it ships its own game files and SteamVR runtime, so you do **not** need the Steam copy of Receiver installed.

## How to launch
Start **SteamVR** first, then either:
- Use the Hub's **Start in VR** button (runs `Receiver.exe`), or
- Use the desktop shortcut **Receiver VR**.

## Install location
Installs to `C:\Games\Receiver VR` (first writable drive: C:/D:/E:).
