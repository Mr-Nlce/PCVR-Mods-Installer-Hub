# Skyrim VR - Wabbajack Improvement Modlist

Skyrim VR is improved through **Wabbajack**, a free automated modlist
installer. Instead of installing individual mods by hand, Wabbajack
downloads and assembles a complete, curated setup for you.

## What you need

- **Skyrim VR** installed on Steam (AppID 611670)
- **Wabbajack** from https://www.wabbajack.org/
- A free **Nexus Mods** account (Wabbajack uses it to fetch mods)

## Mad God Overhaul

A large, curated Skyrim VR modlist that overhauls visuals, gameplay and
VR interaction in one Wabbajack install.

- **Shaders & weather** - choose Azurite 3 or NAT III, full Community Shaders support (PBR, complex grass, volumetric lighting, water reflections)
- **World & seasons** - Season of Skyrim with Nature of the Wildlands 3 and Freaks Floral Fields 2
- **Architecture** - Capital Expansion, Great Cities and JK's Interiors for epic scale
- **Dungeons** - modernized via the Fuzzbeed and Snozz overhauls
- **NPCs & bodies** - realistic (Northbourne/Nithi) or fantasy (Bijin/Pandorable), with CBBE 3BA/HIMBO, BNP skins and OBody diversity
- **Audio** - complete overhaul featuring Lorkhan music and Clofas sounds
- **AI immersion** - pre-installed with unrestricted Mantella AI
- **Mechanics** - Simonrim suite, Open World Loot, Scarcity and SkyTactics
- **New adventures** - Bruma, Vigilant, Wyrmstooth, LOTD and Interesting NPCs
- **VR essentials** - VRIK, Spell Wheel, HIGGS and PLANCK
- Mod page: https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/107780

## How to install

1. Download and run Wabbajack once so it can set itself up.
2. In Wabbajack, open the Gallery and find Mad God Overhaul (or open its `.wabbajack` file).
3. Choose the mod-list installation directory and download directory in Wabbajack - locations with enough free space, separate from the game's own folder.
4. Click Begin and let Wabbajack download and build the modlist.
5. When it finishes, launch the game through the Mod Organizer 2 instance that Wabbajack created in that mod-list installation directory.
