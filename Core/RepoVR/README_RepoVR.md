# R.E.P.O. VR Installer

RepoXR by DaXcess is a fully fledged R.E.P.O. VR mod that adds full 6DoF
motion-controlled VR support. Powered by Unity's OpenXR plugin, so it works
with a wide range of headsets, controllers and runtimes (Oculus, Virtual
Desktop, SteamVR and more).

It's compatible with multiplayer — a lobby can mix flatscreen and VR
players. Running the mod without a headset still lets you see the arm
movements of VR semibots in the lobby, and it stays compatible with vanilla
clients (even if the host has no mods, though there's a small catch in that
case — having the host run the mod improves item physics).

## What it installs
- **BepInEx** — mod loader
- **FixPluginTypesSerialization** — required dependency
- **RepoXR** — VR mod with full motion controls

All packages are downloaded directly from Thunderstore. Running the installer again checks for updates automatically.

## Requirements
- R.E.P.O. owned on Steam
- SteamVR or Virtual Desktop installed

## How to use
Click **Install Mod** on the game tile or detail page and follow the prompts.

## Controls
- **Grab items** — reach out and use Grip (physical hands, not face-targeting)
- **Pull/push held items** — [[Right Stick]] up/down to adjust hold distance
- **Rotate items** — rotate your hand while holding
- **Map** — grab from the toolbelt in front of you (right side) using Grip
- **Dominant hand** — switchable in settings at any time (manual rebind required after switching)
- **Controls rebinding** — Settings → Controls, works in-game and in menus

## Tips
- Eye tracking is supported if your headset has it — enemies that react to gaze will use real eye position
- You can detach your arms from your body for more reach (configurable in settings)
- VR players show a **[VR]** tag in the lobby menu
- A warning appears if the host doesn't have the mod — item rotation may behave oddly in that case

## Known issues
- Other mods may not be VR-compatible — test individually
- Hotswapping (F8) between VR and flat only works from the main menu, not inside a lobby
