# RE Requiem VR

**REFramework** by **praydog**, with the **RE9VR** motion-control add-on by **Talemann** - turns **Resident Evil Requiem (RE9)** into a VR survival-horror experience. Play as FBI analyst Grace Ashcroft investigating the remains of Raccoon City.

> This is an **external** mod: the Hub links you to the biohazardvr.com guide, which has an automatic installer.

## What you get
- VR conversion where every environment - decayed interiors, dark hospital corridors - becomes physically navigable at full scale
- **Motion-controller support** via Talemann's RE9VR add-on
- Survival horror throughout: exploration, puzzles, tense combat, psychological scares

## Requirements
- You must **own Resident Evil Requiem** on Steam
- A PCVR-ready setup with a VR headset
- VR motion controllers (default bindings below)

## How to install (external)
1. Open the info page (biohazardvr.com/re9) - the info button on this card.
2. Use the automatic installer on the page. It auto-detects your Steam copy and guides you through the runtime and setup choices (with backup and uninstall handling).
3. Start the game; it launches in VR.

Manual file-by-file setup is also documented on the page as a fallback.

## Controls (Oculus / Meta Touch - RE9VR default bindings)
These are the official RE9VR default bindings (praydog mod + Talemann VR).

### Left Controller
| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| [[Left Stick]] | Move - click & hold = Run; double-tap down = 180 spin (enable the toggle in the wrist menu first) |
| [[Y]] | Open Inventory |
| [[X]] | Open Map; hold then right dpad = Taunt; Back / Cancel (menu) |
| [[Left Trigger]] | Quick Weapons (hold [[Left Trigger]] + right stick = shortcuts) |
| [[Left Grip]] | Ready Knife/Axe (hold); Grab Ammo; Parry (right before being attacked); Two Hand Support (hold weapon) |

### Right Controller
| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| [[Right Stick]] | Turn - click = Toggle crouch / stand |
| [[B]] | Reload / Eject clip; Quick Throwable (long press); Move Items (in inventory) |
| [[A]] | Interact / Pickup / Select (menu) |
| [[Right Trigger]] | Fire / Attack (when aiming); Attack (quick melee) |
| [[Right Grip]] | Ready Aim (hold); Holster Grab |

**Holsters:** Left Chest = revolvers; Right Hip = pistols; Right Shoulder = long weapons; Left Hip = ammo.

> Parry: raise your left arm with your left palm toward the enemy.
> To use the axe, keep [[Left Grip]] pressed at all times.
> Heal: hold [[Right Grip]] + [[X]] together. Sharpen blade: hold [[Left Grip]] + [[Y]] together.
> Pause: hold both triggers ~3 seconds. Change floor (in map view): press the left or right stick.
> Two-handed weapon support: hold [[Left Grip]] near the gun to grab it as a second hand.
