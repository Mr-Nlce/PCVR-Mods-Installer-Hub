# HL2 VR Ep. Two

The **Half-Life 2: VR Mod** support for **Half-Life 2: Episode Two**, by the **Source VR Mod Team** (includes fholger). The forests, antlions, and Hunters of Ep. Two in full room-scale VR.

> This is an **external** mod: it's free on Steam as part of the HL2 VR Mod project. It requires that you own Half-Life 2: Episode Two.

## What you get
Same VR feature set as the base Half-Life 2: VR Mod:
- Full room-scale VR with tracked motion controls (walk, grab, climb)
- Radial weapon select, over-the-shoulder ammo, functional iron-sights, two-handed weapons, manual reloading
- Left-handed support, laser sight, quick reload
- Smooth/snap turning, head- or controller-oriented movement, motion vignette, teleport option, 3rd-person vehicle camera
- Optional bHaptics and ProVolver haptics

## Requirements
- You must **own Half-Life 2: Episode Two** on Steam (and the HL2 VR Mod)
- A SteamVR-compatible headset

## How to install (external)
1. Open the info page (Steam) and install the Episode Two VR support from the HL2 VR Mod project.
2. Launch with **Start in VR** in the Hub or from Steam; it starts in VR.

## Optional: "Unleashed" addon
**HL2VRU** by **Vittorio Romeo** also works with Episode Two, adding dual wielding, physical melee, a virtual stock, grip-holster mode, and jumping. A workaround for a "White Forest" crash in Ep. Two is included in recent versions. See its page for SteamVR binding setup.
