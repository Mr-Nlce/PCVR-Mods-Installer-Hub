# Half-Life 2 VR

A full room-scale VR conversion of **Half-Life 2** by the **Source VR Mod Team** (a volunteer team that includes fholger). A free Steam mod that translates City 17 into VR in the spirit of Half-Life: Alyx.

> This is an **external** mod: it's a free mod on its own Steam store page. Install it from Steam; it requires that you own Half-Life 2.

## What you get
- Full room-scale VR with tracked motion controls - physically walk, pick up objects, and climb ladders
- Realistic weapon interactions: radial weapon-select menu, over-the-shoulder ammo storage, functional iron-sights, two-handed weapons, manual reloading
- Control options: full left-handed support, laser sight, arcade-style "quick reload"
- Comfort features: smooth or snap turning, head- or controller-oriented movement, motion vignette, 3rd-person vehicle camera, and a teleport movement option
- Optional bHaptics vest and ProVolver haptic-pistol support
- New 3D HUD and remodeled-for-VR weapons; updated maps, models, textures and HDR lighting

## Requirements
- You must **own Half-Life 2** on Steam
- A SteamVR-compatible headset
- The mod leans heavily on single-thread CPU performance

## How to install (external)
1. Open the info page (Steam) and install the free Half-Life 2: VR Mod.
2. Launch it with **Start in VR** in the Hub or from Steam; it starts in VR.

> Controls are fully customizable via SteamVR controller bindings, and many community presets are shared on the Workshop.

## Optional: "Unleashed" addon
The community **HL2VRU (Half-Life 2: VR Mod - Unleashed)** fork by **Vittorio Romeo** adds VR-only extras - dual wielding, physical melee with any weapon or your hands, a virtual stock, grip-holster mode, and jumping. It also works with Episode One and Two. It needs its own SteamVR binding changes; see its page. (Modded HL2VR instances aren't officially supported.)
