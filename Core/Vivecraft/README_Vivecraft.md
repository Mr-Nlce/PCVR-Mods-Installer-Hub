# Vivecraft

Vivecraft is an open-source mod that transforms **Minecraft: Java Edition**
into a fully immersive Virtual Reality experience. It supports major VR
headsets and provides room-scale movement, motion-controlled
interactions, and full multiplayer compatibility with non-VR players.

## What you need

- **Minecraft: Java Edition** (the original PC version - Bedrock is not
  supported)
- A SteamVR-compatible PC VR headset, or a mobile VR device used with a
  SteamVR emulator
- The Vivecraft installer for your Minecraft version - download links per
  version are on the official downloads page

## Supported Minecraft versions

Vivecraft supports Minecraft **1.7.10**, and **1.10 through 1.20.1**.
Individual download links per version are listed on
https://www.vivecraft.org/downloads/

## Hardware

Vivecraft works best in roomscale with SteamVR-compatible hardware such
as the HTC Vive or Cosmos, Valve Index, Oculus Rift S, and WMR headsets.

It can also be played on mobile VR devices such as Oculus Quest, GearVR,
PSVR, Daydream, Cardboard, or Vive Focus via SteamVR emulators.

## Mods, shaders, and modding platforms

- All standalone versions of Vivecraft include **Optifine** for
  performance and shader support
- The **Forge** modding platform is supported on most standalone versions
- Starting with Minecraft 1.18, Vivecraft is also available as a **Fabric
  and Forge compatible mixin mod**
- **Server plugins** are available for Spigot and Forge to enhance your
  VR multiplayer experience

## Multiplayer

Vivecraft works in single player, LAN multi-player, and server
multi-player.

The **Vivecraft NonVR Companion** mod lets your non-VR friends see your
head and hand movements in multiplayer - so even 2D players can see your
VR motions.

## VR features

- **Full roomscale support** - walk around your room as you mine and
  build. Blocks in Minecraft are 1 meter in size, so they're really big
  in VR
- **World scaling** - scale your size in the world up or down; play as an
  ant or a giant
- **Break blocks and fight by swinging your hands**
- **Shoot your bow** by drawing and aiming two-handed
- **Climb, swim, run, jump, sneak, eat, crawl, and row** by actually
  doing the motion
- **Context-sensitive interactions** for doors, pistons, levers, animals,
  buckets and more
- Play with friends and see each other's VR movements (even 2D players,
  via the NonVR Companion)

## Locomotion options

- **Arc teleportation** with optional limitations for a balanced survival
  experience
- **Free-movement** in the direction you point the controller or your
  head
- **Walkabout-style rotation** to use 100% roomscale movement
- **Seated play** with WASD in your look direction

## Streaming

Vivecraft supports **Mixed Reality** capture and undistorted desktop
mirroring for streaming.

## Play style

Optimized for standing play with motion controllers, also supports seated
play with mouse and keyboard.
