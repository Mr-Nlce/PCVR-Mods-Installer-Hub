# RE Village VR

**REFramework** by **praydog** - turns **Resident Evil Village (RE8)** into a VR experience on PC. Hold the line as Ethan Winters across the village, Castle Dimitrescu, and beyond.

> This is an **external** mod: the Hub links you to the biohazardvr.com guide, which has an automatic installer.

## What you get
- VR conversion with head tracking and room-scale presence

## Requirements
- You must **own Resident Evil Village** on Steam
- A PCVR-ready setup with a VR headset
- VR motion controllers (default Touch bindings below)

## How to install (external)
1. Open the info page (biohazardvr.com/re8) - the info button on this card.
2. Use the automatic installer on the page. It auto-detects your Steam copy and guides you through the runtime and setup choices (with backup and uninstall handling).
3. Start the game; it launches in VR.

Manual file-by-file setup is also documented on the page as a fallback.

> The **Shadows of Rose** DLC is covered by NDRW's optional mod, at the end of the linked mod page.

## Controls (Oculus / Meta Touch - praydog mod default bindings)
These are the default VR-mod (motion-controller) bindings. The biohazardvr.com chart also shows an optional community Steam "HoriZon" shared profile in orange (quick-weapon shortcuts, floor change); those extras are not part of the base mod and are omitted here.

### Left Controller
| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| [[Left Stick]] | Move - click & hold = Run |
| [[Y]] | Open Inventory |
| [[X]] | Back / Cancel (menu) |
| [[Left Trigger]] | Quick Weapons |
| [[Left Grip]] | Two Hand Support (hold weapon) |

### Right Controller
| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| [[Right Stick]] | Turn - click = Toggle crouch / stand |
| [[B]] | Reload; Move Items (in inventory) |
| [[A]] | Interact / Pickup / Select (menu) |
| [[Right Trigger]] | Fire / Attack |
| [[Right Grip]] | Aim |

> Swap ammo type: hold [[Right Grip]] + [[A]].
> Map: hold [[Y]] with the controller next to your head / over your shoulder.
> Guard: hold your hands up to your face to take less damage.
> Use recovery items: reach behind your head with your right hand, hold the grip until a medicine bottle appears, then press [[Right Trigger]] to pour.
> Two-handed aiming: hold [[Left Grip]] near the gun to grab it as a second hand.
