# Iron Lung VR Installer

## About this mod

Iron Lung VR is a free, standalone Unity-based VR recreation by Jack
Randolph of the original **Iron Lung** by David Szymanski. The mod is
the whole game - nothing else needs to be owned. Distributed via
itch.io.

Experimental minimalist gameplay where your senses are limited and
anything could be lurking around you. Glimpse your surroundings through
the lens of a grainy still camera and navigate using an incomplete
satellite map and proximity sensors. Horror based around building dread
and paranoia. Original soundtrack inspired by the work of Aubrey Hodges
(Doom 64). A short experience intended to be finished in under an hour.

## What this Hub installer does

- Opens the itch.io download page in your browser
- Waits for you to drag the downloaded `1.2.0.zip` (~150 MB) into the
  installer window
- Extracts the game to `C:\Games\Iron Lung VR` (falls back to
  `D:\Games` / `E:\Games` if `C:\Games` isn't writable)
- Creates an **Iron Lung VR** desktop shortcut

## Requirements

- A free itch.io download (the user grabs the ZIP manually - no
  Steam, no GOG)
- ~600 MB of free disk space on the install drive
- A PCVR headset with SteamVR / OpenVR (the game ships OpenXR +
  Oculus runtimes)
- Start SteamVR before launching

## How to use

Click **Install Mod** on the game tile or detail page and follow the
prompts. When it finishes, start SteamVR, then launch with the **Iron
Lung VR** desktop shortcut.

## Controls

Iron Lung VR uses motion controls - you grip and push the levers and
buttons in the submarine cockpit with your tracked hands. The cockpit
panel is the entire interface: depth gauge, oxygen meter, three
coordinate displays (X / Y / A), satellite map, and the grainy camera
monitor.

- [[Right Trigger]] / [[Left Trigger]] → Grip / press the lever or
  button your hand is touching
- [[Left Stick]] → Tilt forward to move the sub forward when you've
  engaged the throttle lever
- [[Right Stick]] → Rotate the sub when you've engaged the steering
- [[Grip]] (either hand) → Hold to brace / steady yourself

There is no free locomotion - you move by operating the sub's controls
from your seat.
