# Luke Ross R.E.A.L. VR Installer

Automated installer for Luke Ross R.E.A.L. VR — gamepad VR for AAA games.

## How you play
R.E.A.L. VR brings these games into stereoscopic 6DoF VR with full
head-tracking. You play seated with your usual **gamepad or keyboard &
mouse** — the same controls as the flat game, now wrapped around you in
the headset.

## What it installs
- **Luke Ross R.E.A.L. VR v2603.10.1 public** — the VR mod framework

## Requirements
- SteamVR installed
- One or more of the supported games

## How to use
Click **Install Mod** on the game tile or detail page and follow the prompts.

## The in-game R.E.A.L. VR menu

Every R.E.A.L. VR game has a built-in overlay menu where you tune VR
settings live. It's drawn as a flat overlay over the game window, which
shows up inside your headset.

**Opening it:**
- **Keyboard:** press the **Pause/Break** key
- **Gamepad:** press both back buttons together (exact combo can vary by
  controller/game)

**Navigating:** use the D-pad / arrow keys to move, and your gamepad
buttons / Enter to toggle options and move sliders. There's a global
R.E.A.L. VR tab plus a game-specific tab.

**Settings worth tweaking first:**
- **Recentering** — near the top; assign a hotkey/button to quickly realign
  your view. (Some games also let you recenter by briefly shaking your head
  "no".)
- **AER rendering mode** (Advanced) — R.E.A.L. VR uses Alternate Eye
  Rendering, so a steady framerate matters. Switching to **Mono** removes
  ghosting/eye-flip artifacts entirely and boosts performance on mid-range
  GPUs (at the cost of stereo depth).
- **HUD scale & distance** — shrink the HUD (e.g. ~0.6) so map/crosshair/UI
  text isn't cut off at the edge of your lenses.
- **Resolution / Target Pixels Per Degree** — adjust render resolution,
  then hit **Optimize Resolution** right next to it so the change takes
  effect. Don't set it super high; it hits performance hard.
