# Resident Evil 2R VR

**REFramework** by **praydog** - turns **Resident Evil 2 (Remake)** into a full first-person VR survival-horror experience on PC. Step directly into the streets and corridors of Raccoon City.

> This is an **external** mod: the Hub links you to the biohazardvr.com guide, which has an automatic installer.

## What you get
- Full first-person VR conversion with stereoscopic rendering
- Room-scale and head tracking: lean around corners, peek through doorways, inspect dark rooms naturally
- One of the most-praised unofficial VR horror experiences - the remake's tight corridors and tension translate extremely well

## Requirements
- You must **own Resident Evil 2 (Remake)** on Steam
- A PCVR-ready setup with a VR headset
- VR motion controllers (default Touch bindings below)

## How to install (external)
1. Open the info page (biohazardvr.com/re2) - the info button on this card.
2. Use the automatic installer on the page. It auto-detects your Steam copy and guides you through the runtime and setup choices (with backup and uninstall handling).
3. Start the game; it launches in VR.

Manual file-by-file setup is also documented on the page as a fallback.

## Controls (Oculus / Meta Touch - praydog mod default bindings)
These are the default VR-mod (motion-controller) bindings. The biohazardvr.com chart also shows an optional community Steam "HoriZon" shared profile in orange; those extras are not part of the base mod and are omitted here.

### Left Controller
| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| [[Left Stick]] | Move - click & hold = Run |
| [[Y]] | Open Inventory |
| [[X]] | Back / Cancel (menu) |
| [[Left Trigger]] | Quick Weapons; Counter when grabbed (if holding a melee weapon) |
| [[Left Grip]] | Ready Sub-Weapon (hold); Two Hand Support (hold while weapon readied) |

### Right Controller
| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| [[Right Stick]] | Turn |
| [[B]] | Reload; Move Items (in inventory) |
| [[A]] | Interact / Pickup / Select (menu) |
| [[Right Trigger]] | Fire / Attack |
| [[Right Grip]] | Ready Weapon (hold); Prime Grenade (hold & throw) |

> Map: hold [[Y]] with the controller next to your head / over your shoulder.
> Two-handed aiming: hold [[Right Grip]] to ready the weapon, bring your other hand near the gun and hold [[Left Grip]].
