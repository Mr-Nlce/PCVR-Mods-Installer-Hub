# Devil May Cry 5 VR Installer

Automated installer for **REFramework VR** by praydog — generic 6DOF VR
support for DMC5, the RE Engine hack-and-slash. You play DMC5's stylish
combat on a huge stereoscopic 3D screen-in-VR with full head tracking.

## What it installs
- **REFramework Nightly v01320** — extracts directly into the DMC5 game folder

## Features

- **Generic 6DOF VR** with full head tracking — look around Devil Hunter
  combat in stereoscopic 3D
- **Free camera**, **FOV slider** and **timescale** controls via the
  REFramework overlay menu
- Configurable in real time through REFramework's in-game menu (no game
  files are modified)

## Note

DMC5's fast third-person combat is built around a gamepad, so VR here is
gamepad-driven (see Controls below) rather than motion-controlled. One
known REFramework quirk: in-VR audio positioning doesn't track your
headset rotation.

## How to use

Click **Install Mod** on the game tile or detail page and follow the prompts.
The installer asks for your VR platform and handles the setup automatically.

## VR Platform

**SteamVR** — Quest via Link/Air Link, Valve Index, and other SteamVR headsets.
No extra steps needed.

**OpenXR** — Oculus/Meta PC app, or Virtual Desktop in OpenXR mode.
The installer automatically removes `openvr_api.dll` to switch to OpenXR.

## Controls

This mod uses **gamepad controls only** — no motion controls.

- Use a physical gamepad, or
- Virtual Desktop users: in the VD **Input** tab, enable **"Use touch controllers as gamepad"**
