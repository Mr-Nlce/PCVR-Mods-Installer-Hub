# Fallout 4 VR - Wabbajack Improvement Modlists

Fallout 4 VR is improved through **Wabbajack**, a free automated modlist
installer. Instead of installing individual mods by hand, Wabbajack
downloads and assembles a complete, curated setup for you.

## What you need

- **Fallout 4 VR** installed on Steam (AppID 611660)
- **Wabbajack** from https://www.wabbajack.org/
- A free **Nexus Mods** account (Wabbajack uses it to fetch mods)

## Fallout 4 VR Essentials

A complete VR overhaul focused on visuals, immersion and stability.

- **Full body & holsters** - the latest FRIK with a visible body and holsters
- **HD visuals** - high-res textures, forest overhaul, optimized textures, darker nights
- **Seasons** - custom edited seasonal change, with desolate and high-performance variants
- **Haptics** - TrueGear or bHaptics suit integration
- **Stability** - optimized Commonwealth and interiors, DLC fixes
- **Survival quality-of-life** - auto eat/drink, manual saves, settlement fast travel
- **VR interaction** - hand model, improved Pip-Boy handling, customizable HUD, bullet time
- **Accurate VR weapons** plus many new working guns
- **Settlements** - Sim Settlements and Conqueror, auto-built and raidable (customizable)
- **Smarter AI** - smart enemies and companions, companion whistle, traveling NPCs, stalkers
- **Survival feel** - reduced loot, live dismemberment, non-bullet-sponge enemies
- **New content** - quests, locations, weather (True Storms, Vivid Weather), radio, music
- **Optional AI NPCs** via Mantella
- Mod page: https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/96013

## Fallout London VR

A VR-ready setup for the Fallout London total-conversion - a ruined London
to explore in the Fallout universe, overhauling Fallout 4 with a brand new
story and world.

- **200 quests** to shape London's future
- **20 factions** fighting for power
- **7 companions** to travel with
- **15 boroughs** built from the ground up
- **Extended dialogue system** for player-driven choices
- **Original soundtrack** and full voice acting for major characters
- **7 unique settlements** to build
- New craftable items, many new weapons, and dynamic new animations
- Mod page: https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4london/mods/747

## How to install

1. Download and run Wabbajack once so it can set itself up.
2. In Wabbajack, open the Gallery and select the modlist you want (or open its `.wabbajack` file).
3. Choose the mod-list installation directory and download directory in Wabbajack - locations with enough free space, separate from the game's own folder.
4. Click Begin and let Wabbajack download and build the modlist.
5. When it finishes, launch the game through the Mod Organizer 2 instance that Wabbajack created in that mod-list installation directory.
