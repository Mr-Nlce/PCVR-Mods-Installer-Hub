# 7 Days to Die VR Mod Installer

Automated installer for 7DaysVR.

## What it does
1. Locates your 7 Days to Die installation automatically
2. Opens Nexus Mods in your browser so you can download the mod
3. Finds the downloaded ZIP and installs it into the game folder
4. Opens the 7 Days Launcher so you can disable EasyAntiCheat
5. Patches video settings for VR (no TAA, no reflections, fullscreen on)

## Requirements
- Windows 10/11
- Steam with **7 Days to Die** installed
- SteamVR installed
- A Nexus Mods account (free) to download the mod

## Mod download
https://www.nexusmods.com/7daystodie/mods/3011

## Important notes
- **EAC must be disabled** — you can only join servers with EAC also off
- **7 Days must be the active foreground window** while playing — disable SteamVR Always on Top
- World loading screen is very jittery — this is normal (CPU at 100%)
- VR options in-game: Options -> Controls -> VR (far right tab)
- Recalibrate height: stand straight, hold both thumbsticks for 2 seconds

## Controls

![Controller layout](ControllerLayout.jpg)

Default VR controls. All of these can be remapped via standard SteamVR
controller bindings if you prefer.

**Left controller:**
- **[[Left Stick]]:** Move; press = Run
- **[[Left Trigger]]:** Next slot / zoom in
- **[[Left Grip]]:** Previous slot / zoom out
- **[[X]]:** Inventory
- **[[Y]]:** Menu

**Right controller:**
- **[[Right Stick]]:** Left/Right = Turn (smooth or snap); Up = Map;
  Down = Toggle Crouch; press = Reload / Take all
- **[[Right Trigger]]:** Primary (fire / use / left-click and drag in menus)
- **[[Right Grip]]:** melee weapons = hold for power attack or repair/upgrade;
  other items = Secondary (aim down sights / right-click)
- **[[A]]:** Jump / full click in UI
- **[[B]]:** Interact / exit vehicle

## Physical swings & power attacks
- Physical swings with motion controllers work for melee weapons & tools.
- Hold **[[Right Grip]]** while swinging (or press **[[Right Trigger]]**) for a
  power attack.
- Power attack on the stone axe = repair/upgrade. You can't repair trees or
  zombies.

## Radial menus
- Hold **[[X]]** for the 'Tab' radial (miscellaneous menus)
- Hold **[[Right Stick]]** (as a button) for the 'R' radial (build shapes, ammo
  selection)
- Hold **[[B]]** for the 'E' radial (interaction menus, e.g. add fuel / open
  vehicle inventory)

## Handy default actions
- **Aim down sights** is on **[[Right Grip]]** for ranged weapons: you'll hear
  a click and your stamina drains slowly — there's no other visual cue, but
  accuracy and recoil improve a lot. Recommended for all ranged weapons.
- **Full click in menus:** press **[[A]]** instead of the trigger — easier than
  holding the controller still.
- **Toggle flashlight:** hold **[[Right Grip]]** and press **[[Right Stick]]**.
- **Transfer item between containers:** hold **[[Left Grip]]** and press **[[A]]**.
- **Auto-stack into a container:** with a container open, hold **both grips**
  and press **[[A]]**.
- **Honk horn:** in a vehicle, hold **[[Left Grip]]** and press **[[Left Stick]]**.
