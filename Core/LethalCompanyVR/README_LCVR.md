# Lethal Company VR Installer

Ready to immerse yourself in the horrors of Lethal Company in VR? LCVR by
DaXcess adds full 6DOF VR with hand movement and motion-based controls.
Powered by Unity's OpenXR plugin, so it works with a wide range of
headsets, controllers and runtimes (Oculus, Virtual Desktop, SteamVR and
more). Works seamlessly in multiplayer with VR and non-VR players in the
same lobby — even with vanilla clients, and even if the host has no mods.

## What it installs
**Option 1 — Current version (auto-updates):**
- BepInEx, FixPluginTypesSerialization, TypeLoadExceptionFixer, LethalCompanyVR — all from Thunderstore

**Option 2 — Legacy pinned (LCVR 1.4.6 / V73):**
- Same packages at pinned versions, requires switching to the "previous" Steam branch

## Requirements
- Lethal Company owned on Steam
- SteamVR or Virtual Desktop installed

## How to use
Click **Install Mod** on the game tile or detail page and select your preferred option. The installer checks Thunderstore for the current mod status and shows whether it's deprecated.

## Tips
- The host does not need the VR mod for VR players to see each other's hand movements
- Non-VR players in the same lobby are fully compatible

## Physical VR interactions

LCVR replaces boring laser-pointer interactions with physical ones (each
can be toggled off in the config):
- **Ship lever:** physically pull/push it to take off and land
- **Monitor buttons:** moved next to the lever — press them to switch the
  radar view
- **Charging station:** just hold a battery item up to it (right hand only)
- **Ship door / teleporter:** smash the buttons; flick open the teleporter
  cover and punch the button
- **Company bell & ship horn:** tap the bell, pull the horn cord
- **Breaker box:** flick the door open, flip switches with your pointer finger
- **Doors:** grab the handle to open; use a key or lockpicker with your
  right hand while holding it
- **Face items:** hold a walkie or similar up to your face to use it (right
  hand). **Muffle** your voice by covering your mouth — enemies can't hear
  you (but your vision narrows the longer you hold it)

## VR additions

- **Spray paint:** physically shake the can
- **Shovel / sign:** held in two hands — swing down from over your shoulder
  to attack
- **Knife:** swing it to slice
- **Maneater baby:** gently shake to calm it (shake too hard at your peril)
- **Belt bag:** hold it upside down and shake to drop items

## Terminal & menus

In the terminal, a virtual keyboard appears (with quick CONFIRM/DENY
macros) since you have no physical keyboard. Menus use ray interactors —
point and pull the trigger.

## Known issues
- Some game updates break compatibility — the installer will show **[DEPRECATED]** next to Option 1 when this happens, at which point Option 2 is the safe choice
